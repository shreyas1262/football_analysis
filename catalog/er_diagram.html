Three join keys, one anchor. Every match-grain table carries match_id as an FK back to SCHEDULE (one match → two team rows, hence one-to-many). Within a family, the stat tables join one-to-one on their shared key — player_id, (team_id, season), or team_match_id. The keeper tables are zero-or-one: only goalkeepers appear (212 player-keeper rows vs 2,854 standard).
